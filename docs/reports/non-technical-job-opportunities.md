# Non-Technical Job Opportunities

This document is based on the business model and launch assumptions in
`docs/reports/repository-to-startup-analysis-report.md`.

The product is a governed runbook execution platform for SRE, platform, and
operations teams. A person without technical or engineering skills can still
contribute meaningfully, especially in customer discovery, sales support,
onboarding, documentation, operations, and customer success.

## Best Early Opportunities

| Job | Why it fits the business model | Main responsibilities | When useful |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer discovery coordinator | The report recommends interviewing 20-30 platform and SRE leaders before launch. | Find prospects, schedule interviews, track notes, summarize patterns, maintain CRM records. | Pre-launch |
| Founder-led sales assistant | Early pilots rely on simple pilot pricing and direct selling. | Build lead lists, prepare outreach, follow up with prospects, manage calendars, update pipeline status. | Pre-launch through launch |
| Pilot onboarding coordinator | First customers need high-touch setup and weekly check-ins. | Coordinate pilot kickoff, gather customer goals, schedule check-ins, collect success metrics, document blockers. | Launch |
| Customer success associate | The product will need proof that teams are getting value from workflows, approvals, and evidence capture. | Track customer usage, collect feedback, help users navigate workflows, escalate product issues. | Launch through post-launch |
| Operations documentation writer | The report calls out operational docs, support/debug tooling, and compliance documentation as launch needs. | Write user guides, onboarding checklists, support articles, FAQ pages, and pilot playbooks. | Pre-launch through post-launch |
| Sales development representative | The ideal customer is a 20-300 engineer software company with a platform or SRE team. | Research target accounts, identify likely buyers, send outbound messages, book demos. | Launch |
| Product marketing generalist | The product should sell controlled operational execution, not generic AI automation. | Write positioning, landing page copy, case studies, pilot summaries, and comparison messaging. | Launch through post-launch |
| Customer research analyst | Roadmap priority should come from actual runbooks and pilot usage. | Organize interview notes, tag recurring pain points, prepare customer insight summaries. | Pre-launch through post-launch |

## Useful Support Roles

| Job | Why it helps | Main responsibilities |
|---|---|---|
| Administrative assistant | Founder time is a major bottleneck across sales, support, and product work. | Scheduling, inbox triage, meeting notes, travel, vendor coordination. |
| CRM and pipeline coordinator | Pilot sales need consistent follow-up and clean records. | Maintain CRM data, track next steps, prepare weekly pipeline reports. |
| Demo coordinator | The product is demo-capable before it is fully sellable. | Schedule demos, prepare agendas, capture questions, send follow-up materials. |
| Support coordinator | Early customers will need responsive help even before a formal support team exists. | Triage support requests, maintain issue logs, coordinate responses with technical staff. |
| Compliance documentation assistant | Enterprise buyers will ask about security, audit, access control, and data handling. | Maintain questionnaires, collect policy drafts, organize evidence, prepare customer-facing packets. |
| Billing and account operations associate | Later monetization includes subscriptions, seats, usage limits, and enterprise contracts. | Prepare invoices, track renewals, maintain account records, coordinate contract paperwork. |
| Recruiting coordinator | The report recommends early hires across backend, frontend, infra, product/design, and GTM. | Source candidates, schedule interviews, manage applicant tracking, prepare offer logistics. |
| Community and event coordinator | SRE and platform buyers can be reached through focused communities and events. | Coordinate webinars, conference logistics, newsletters, and customer roundtables. |

## Later-Stage Opportunities

| Job | Why it becomes relevant later | Main responsibilities |
|---|---|---|
| Account executive | Once pilots convert repeatably, sales can become a dedicated function. | Run discovery calls, manage opportunities, negotiate pilots and annual contracts. |
| Customer success manager | Larger customers will need adoption plans and renewal management. | Own accounts, run business reviews, track outcomes, reduce churn. |
| Implementation project manager | Private cloud, self-hosted, and enterprise onboarding will require coordination. | Manage timelines, dependencies, customer stakeholders, and launch readiness checklists. |
| Partner manager | Integrations and deployment partners may matter after product-market fit. | Manage relationships with consultants, cloud partners, and integration vendors. |
| Content marketer | Once the ideal customer profile is validated, repeatable education can generate demand. | Produce articles, guides, webinars, customer stories, and newsletters. |
| Revenue operations specialist | Pricing later includes platform fees, seats, usage tiers, and enterprise add-ons. | Maintain sales systems, reporting, plan rules, usage tracking, and renewal workflows. |

## Lower-Fit Roles For The Current Stage

These roles may become useful later, but they are not the best first options for
a person without technical or engineering skills:

- Dedicated brand marketer before the ideal customer profile is validated.
- Large-scale paid advertising manager before sales messaging is proven.
- Enterprise sales leader before the product is sellable and pilots are repeatable.
- Full-time event marketer before there are case studies or repeatable demos.
- Full-time compliance manager before customers require formal compliance programs.

## Best Match By Stage

| Stage | Strongest non-technical jobs |
|---|---|
| Pre-launch | Customer discovery coordinator, sales assistant, research analyst, documentation writer |
| First pilots | Pilot onboarding coordinator, customer success associate, demo coordinator, support coordinator |
| Repeatable launch | Sales development representative, product marketing generalist, account executive, CRM coordinator |
| Enterprise expansion | Customer success manager, implementation project manager, compliance documentation assistant, revenue operations specialist |

## Practical Starting Point

The most valuable first non-technical role is a combined customer discovery,
sales support, and pilot operations generalist.

That person should focus on:

- Finding and scheduling conversations with platform, SRE, infrastructure, and engineering operations leaders.
- Capturing pain points around runbooks, approvals, incidents, release processes, and compliance evidence.
- Maintaining a simple CRM.
- Coordinating pilot setup and follow-up.
- Turning customer conversations into clear notes for product and sales decisions.
